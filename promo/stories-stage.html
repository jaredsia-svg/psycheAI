1Request your Instagram data
  1. a.Open the Instagram app and go to your Profile
  2. b.Tap ≡ top right, then Accounts Centre
  3. c.Tap Your information and permissions
  4. d.Tap Export your information, then Create export
  5. e.Choose your Instagram account, then Export to device
  6. f.Set Date range All time, Format JSON, Media quality Lower
  7. g.Tap Start export and enter your password
2Load it at psycheai.io

Instagram emails you a link, usually in 1–2 hours. Download the .zip, then tap Load my data and get your Psyche Card.

🔒 Privacy · 1 / 3

Your files never leave your phone.

Does my Instagram file get uploaded?
No.

Your zip, photos and videos are read in your browser. Only a short, de-identified summary of your activity is sent for analysis, and you review it first.

Can the summary be traced back to me?
It's de-identified on your phone.
  • Your name and username become a blank
  • Emails, phone and card numbers are removed
  • Other people's @handles become [P1]
  • Links dropped; dates cut to the month
🔒 Privacy · 2 / 3
Can anyone else access my data? What if PsycheAI gets hacked?
No one can.

No account, and PsycheAI stores none of your data, summary or report. Even its administrator can't see them. If PsycheAI were hacked, there would be none of your data to take.

Is my data used to train the AI model?
No.

It runs on Google Vertex AI's enterprise tier, where Google does not train its AI on it. It's used only to answer you.

Do I need to enter my password anywhere?
Only in Instagram.

Instagram asks for it when you request your data in its app. PsycheAI never asks for a password and never logs in to your accounts.

There are no accounts: your card and report stay only on your device.

🔒 Privacy · 3 / 3
How do I know it's true?
Check it yourself. ✈️

Turn on airplane mode, then load your file: it's still read and summarised, because that all happens on your phone.

The site is open source: anyone can read exactly what it sends.

Why not just upload my data to ChatGPT?
That sends the raw file.

Your whole export goes to their servers, names and messages included. PsycheAI de-identifies it on your phone first, and keeps nothing.
