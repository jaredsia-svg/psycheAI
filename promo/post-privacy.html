How your data flows

Your files are read on your phone. Only a short, de‑identified summary ever leaves it.

  1. 1
    Your data files stay on your phoneYour Instagram, WhatsApp, Google or Facebook files are opened right in your browser.
  2. 2
    A short summary is made on your phoneJust 100–200 KB of text. Names, handles, emails and numbers are removed. You check it first.
  3. 3
    Only that summary is sent, encryptedTo Google Vertex AI enterprise, which never trains on it.
  4. 4
    Your results come back to your phoneYour card, report and Syncs are saved only there. PsycheAI keeps no copy.
