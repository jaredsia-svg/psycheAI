# ESTJ: The practical leader

ESTJs organise, decide and keep things on track. They value structure, honesty and getting results.

## The four letters

- **E – Extraversion**: energised by people and activity
- **S – Sensing**: focused on the concrete and practical
- **T – Thinking**: decides with logic and consistency first
- **J – Judging**: likes plans, decisions and closure

## How ESTJ shows up in your data

- Posts about achievements and events
- Running group chats and plans
- Quick, clear replies

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Organised
- Decisive
- Reliable

### Worth watching

- Flexibility when plans change
- Making room for quieter voices
