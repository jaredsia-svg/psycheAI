The Psyche personality test

Most personality tests ask you questions about yourself. The Psyche personality test asks none: it reads how you actually behave in your own Instagram data, and shows the evidence for every result.

By Jared, founder of PsycheAI · Updated 26 October 2026

What it measures

  • MBTI: your four letters, each with how clearly it showed (slight, moderate or clear). The 16 types.
  • The Big Five: openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness and emotional sensitivity, each scored 0 to 100. More on the Big Five.
  • Love languages: how you show care, and in the Psyche Report how you like to receive it. More on love languages.
  • Values, motivations and interests: what you stand for and what you keep coming back to.
  • Your patterns: three habits of yours, named, with the evidence behind each.
  • The character you are most like, by temperament, never by looks. Which character are you?

How it works

  1. Request your data from Instagram. It takes about 30 seconds, and the file usually arrives within 1–2 hours. Step-by-step guide. Or export two or three WhatsApp chats, which takes seconds.
  2. Load the file in PsycheAI. It is read in your own browser and reduced to a short, de-identified digest of your own activity, which you can review.
  3. Your Psyche Card is written from that digest, with a signal strength score saying how much evidence there was.

Why no questions

A questionnaire measures how you see yourself on the day you answer it. Years of your own posts, comments and messages show what you actually do. That is harder to fake and often more interesting, because it can show the patterns you would not think to mention.

Card, report and coach

Your free Psyche Card is a shareable summary of all of the above. The Psyche Report explains every part with the evidence, and adds your wellbeing, attachment style, career strengths and a development plan. Ask PsycheAI then answers your own questions about it, as a coach that has read your report.

Questions

Is the Psyche personality test free?

Yes. Your first Psyche Card and Psyche Report are free, together.

How long does it take?

About a minute once your Instagram data has arrived. Requesting it takes about 30 seconds.

Is it accurate?

It is an inference from behaviour, not a clinical measurement, and it says how confident it is. More of your own writing gives a stronger signal; you can add WhatsApp chats or Google and Facebook data to sharpen it.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account. Your results stay in your browser until you delete them.
