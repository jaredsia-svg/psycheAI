What a Buzz Lightyear reading means

A Buzz Lightyear reading is someone with a strong sense of purpose and the confidence to go after it. They like a goal, a plan and a team, and they bring energy and optimism to all three. Their best moments come when they pair that drive with a sense of humour about themselves.

You might be most like Buzz Lightyear if…

  • You post goals, training and progress.
  • Your saves include challenges, gear and things to master.
  • You rally people to join you.
  • You bounce back quickly from setbacks.

Strengths and what to watch
