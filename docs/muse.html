Psyche Pair, if you want it

One tap after your Psyche. Muse shows you the card matches will see, you fix anything that's off, and it asks the few things Instagram can't show, like your religion and plans for kids. Then, once a week, the few people who fit you best: on what you need, how you live, where you're heading, how you talk things through and what you value. Pick up to two. If it's mutual, message each other in Muse or swap Instagram.

No swiping. If no one fits well enough this week, we show no one. Cards only ever say what's good about someone.

Privacy

Muse reads your Instagram on your behalf; we never receive your posts, captions, comments, stories or messages. Your Psyche is sealed under a key only your link has. If you join Psyche Pair or use Sync, we keep your card-level profile, encrypted, until you delete it. The full privacy policy.

Terms

18 and over; your own Instagram and real, recent photos; be decent. Insight and coaching, never therapy or a prediction. The full terms.

Safety

Before you meet. Chat first. Meet somewhere public, tell a friend where you'll be, and get yourself there and back.

Red flags. Requests for money, gifts or crypto; pressure to move to another app quickly; a story that changes; refusing a video call before meeting.

Report or block. Say to Muse: "Block and report <name>", with a reason. Blocking removes you from each other's matches and ends your chat.

In danger? Call 999 in Singapore, or your local emergency number.
