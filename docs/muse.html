Add it to Muse

  1. In Muse, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, or ask Muse to "add a custom connector".
  2. Paste this address: https://app.psycheai.io/mcp
  3. Sign in with Google, Apple or your email (we send a 6-digit code), then say "Read my Psyche".

Muse may say it hasn't reviewed this connector. Until Meta's review, PsycheAI runs as a custom connector you add yourself.

Privacy

Muse reads your Instagram on your behalf; we never receive your posts, captions, comments, stories or messages. Your read is sealed under a key only your link has. If you join Psyche Pair or use Sync, we keep your card-level profile, encrypted, until you delete it. The full privacy policy.

Terms

18 and over; your own Instagram and real, recent photos; be decent. Insight and coaching, never therapy or a prediction. The full terms.

Safety

Before you meet. Chat first. Meet somewhere public, tell a friend where you'll be, and get yourself there and back.

Red flags. Requests for money, gifts or crypto; pressure to move to another app quickly; a story that changes; refusing a video call before meeting.

Report or block. Say to Muse: "Block and report <name>", with a reason. Blocking removes you from each other's matches and ends your chat.

In danger? Call 999 in Singapore, or your local emergency number.
