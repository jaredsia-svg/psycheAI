How it works Report an issue
  1. Paste it into Muse and send.
  2. When Muse asks to share information with app.psycheai.io, tap Always allow this site, so it asks only once. It covers app.psycheai.io only, PsycheAI's own server, which the skill asks for its account, menus, card art and signed updates. There's no sign-up.
  3. Choose whether to include your chat messages. Your Psyche is ready in about 5 minutes.

PsycheAI installs only from app.psycheai.io (psycheai.io/muse is its short link), and every update is signed. You can check your copy against the public code on GitHub. Anything else isn't PsycheAI.

What PsycheAI can do

Reads
Your Instagram posts, stories and comments, and your chat messages if you choose. WhatsApp, Facebook or Messenger only if you add them. All in Muse, on your own account, de-identified first.
Never
Posts, comments, likes, follows, or messages anyone without your tap.
Shares
Only when you choose Sync, and then only your card.
Costs
Nothing. PsycheAI in Muse is free, fresh reads included. The prices on psycheai.io are for reads made on the website.
Keeps
None of your posts, messages or report: your Psyche Card and report are made in Muse. Delete it all any time: say "Delete my PsycheAI data" in Muse.
