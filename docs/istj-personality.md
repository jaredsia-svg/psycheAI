# ISTJ: The dependable steward

ISTJs are reliable, thorough and loyal. They value doing things properly and keeping their word.

## The four letters

- **I – Introversion**: energised by time alone and close company
- **S – Sensing**: focused on the concrete and practical
- **T – Thinking**: decides with logic and consistency first
- **J – Judging**: likes plans, decisions and closure

## How ISTJ shows up in your data

- Regular, predictable activity
- Practical saves and searches
- Long-standing friendships

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Dependable
- Thorough
- Loyal

### Worth watching

- Trying new approaches
- Sharing feelings as well as facts
