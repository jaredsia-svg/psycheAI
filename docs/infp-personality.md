# INFP: The gentle dreamer

INFPs are guided by personal values and a rich inner world. They are creative, sincere and kind.

## The four letters

- **I – Introversion**: energised by time alone and close company
- **N – Intuition**: drawn to ideas, patterns and possibilities
- **F – Feeling**: decides with people and values first
- **P – Perceiving**: likes open options and adapting as they go

## How INFP shows up in your data

- Creative or reflective posts
- Saves of art, writing and music
- Close, heartfelt conversations

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Creative
- Empathetic
- True to their values

### Worth watching

- Turning ideas into routines
- Speaking up in groups
