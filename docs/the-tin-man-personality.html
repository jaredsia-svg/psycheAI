What a The Tin Man reading means

A Tin Man reading is someone whose kindness runs deeper than they give themselves credit for. They are patient and steady, show care through what they do, and stay loyal to the people and things that matter to them. They often worry about whether they feel enough, which is usually the surest sign that they do.

You might be most like The Tin Man if…

  • You check on friends quietly, without making a show of it.
  • You keep things that remind you of people.
  • You stay in touch with a small, steady circle for years.
  • Your interests run deep rather than wide.
