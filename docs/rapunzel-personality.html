What a Rapunzel reading means

A Rapunzel reading is someone who fills their days with making things and noticing things. They are open, kind and quick to be delighted, and they keep a long list of what they want to try next. Their optimism is not naive: it is a choice they make about how to see things.

You might be most like Rapunzel if…

  • Your saves are full of art, craft, recipes and ideas.
  • You comment warmly and often on other people’s work.
  • You keep a running list of places and things to try.
  • Your captions are bright, curious and a little playful.

Strengths
