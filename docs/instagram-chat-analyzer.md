# An Instagram chat analyzer that reads you

The short answer: most chat analyzers take one conversation and count it: who texts more, who replies faster, a score. PsycheAI reads your side of all your direct messages, with your posts and comments, on your own device, and says what they show about you: how you keep up with people, how you show care and how you handle a disagreement.

## What it reads in your DMs

- **How many people you keep up with**, and how many of those conversations are still going.
- **How much of each conversation is you**: what you send against what you receive.
- **How you write**: the length of your messages, and a sample of your own words spread across the years, not just last month.
- **What you are like with people**: warmth, humour, the plans you make and how you answer when a conversation turns tense.

## Your side, de-identified

Your download is read on your own phone or computer, and the zip never leaves it. The summary holds a sample of your own messages, some with a short, de-identified line of what they answered so the reading makes sense. The names of the people you message become numbers like [N1], and links, emails and phone numbers are removed. Before anything is sent you see which chats are in it, and can switch any of them off. [How your data is handled](https://psycheai.io/faq).

## More than a chat analyzer

A chat analyzer describes one conversation. PsycheAI reads your whole account, DMs, posts, comments and what you keep coming back to, and gives you a free Psyche Card: your MBTI, Big Five, love languages, values and three patterns of yours, each with its evidence. To compare yourself with a friend, [Psyche Sync](https://psycheai.io/best-friend-compatibility-test) reads both of your cards, never your messages.

## How to get your messages

Instagram has no export button inside a chat. Request your data download instead: Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions → Download your information, with Format set to **JSON** and Date range to **All time**. It takes about 30 seconds, and the file usually arrives within 1–2 hours. [The step-by-step guide](https://psycheai.io/download-instagram-data).

## Questions

### Does it read the other person’s messages?

They are read on your device to follow the conversation. What is sent is a sample of your own messages, some with a short, de-identified line of what they answered, and the other person’s name becomes a number.

### Can I analyse just one chat?

PsycheAI reads your whole account rather than one chat, because one conversation says more about that friendship than about you. In the review you can switch off any chat you would rather leave out.

### Does it give a compatibility score?

No. Psyche Sync compares two people’s cards and explains where you align and where you differ, without a score. It is for friends and family, never romance.

Yes. Your first Psyche Card and Psyche Report are free.
