# INTP: The curious analyst

INTPs are driven by curiosity about how things work. They enjoy ideas for their own sake, question assumptions and follow interests wherever they lead.

## The four letters

- **I – Introversion**: energised by time alone and close company
- **N – Intuition**: drawn to ideas, patterns and possibilities
- **T – Thinking**: decides with logic and consistency first
- **P – Perceiving**: likes open options and adapting as they go

## How INTP shows up in your data

- Saves and searches that jump between deep interests
- Late-night activity during a new rabbit hole
- Thoughtful, sometimes long messages to a few people

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Original thinking
- Open-minded
- Good at untangling problems

### Worth watching

- Finishing what they start
- Saying the caring thing out loud
