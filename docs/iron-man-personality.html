What a Iron Man reading means

An Iron Man reading is someone with big ideas and the confidence to build them. They are quick, inventive and at home with technology and challenges. They do their best work when that brilliance is shared with a team they trust.

You might be most like Iron Man if…

  • Your saves and searches are full of tech, tools and new ideas.
  • You share opinions confidently.
  • You work in intense bursts.
  • You like to fix and improve things.
