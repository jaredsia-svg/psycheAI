# How in sync are you with your best friend?

Psyche Sync compares two Psyche Cards and shows where you align, where you differ and how each of you can relate better to the other. For best friends, siblings, cousins or anyone close.

## How it works

1. Each of you makes a free Psyche Card from your own Instagram data.
2. One of you sends the other their personal link.
3. One tap shows your Psyche Sync. You can choose to share the result with them.

## What it compares

Your energy and how much contact you each like, your shared interests, your values, how each of you shows care, and how you each handle a disagreement. Each point comes with the evidence from both cards.

## For friends and family alike

The reading never assumes what you are to each other. It works the same for a best friend, a sibling or a parent, and it is never romantic.

## No score, no verdict

There is no percentage and no ranking. Differences are written as differences, each with a way to make it work, so it is something you can share without anyone feeling judged.

## Questions

### Is it free?

Yes. Psyche Sync is free, and so is each person’s first Psyche Card.

### Does my friend see my data?

No. Your link carries only your card’s short summary, never your data, messages or report.

### Can I do it with my sister or brother?

Yes. Psyche Sync is for any two people who know each other.
