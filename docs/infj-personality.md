# INFJ: The insightful idealist

INFJs combine strong values with a deep understanding of people. They are private, purposeful and care about meaning.

## The four letters

- **I – Introversion**: energised by time alone and close company
- **N – Intuition**: drawn to ideas, patterns and possibilities
- **F – Feeling**: decides with people and values first
- **J – Judging**: likes plans, decisions and closure

## How INFJ shows up in your data

- Few, considered posts
- Long, warm messages with close friends
- Saves about growth, meaning and causes

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Insight into people
- Principled
- Deeply caring

### Worth watching

- Sharing their own needs
- Resting before burning out
