Why skip the questionnaire?

Self-report tests have a well-known problem: the answers drift with mood, with how the question is worded, and with the version of yourself you would like to be. Retake one a month later and a letter can flip. Behaviour is steadier. How you spend your evenings, whether you broadcast or converse, whether you plan or improvise, how you write to people close to you: years of that is a better record of type than ten minutes of agreeing or disagreeing.

What you get

  • Your Big Five: openness, conscientiousness, agreeableness, extraversion and sensitivity, each from 0 to 100.
  • The evidence behind each reading, from your own activity.

How to start

  1. In Instagram, open Settings → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Choose Create export → your account → Export to device.
  3. Set Date range to All time, Format to JSON (not HTML) and Media quality to Lower.
  4. Instagram emails a link, usually within a few hours. Download the .zip and load it into PsycheAI.

What happens to your data

Your Instagram zip, your photos and videos, the finished report and other people’s messages — apart from a short, anonymised line of what some of your messages were replying to — never leave your device. A short digest of your own words and activity is de-identified first (your name becomes a blank; emails, phone and ID numbers are removed; other people’s handles become numbers like [P1]) and is sent once to Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude to write the reading. PsycheAI keeps no copy, and there is no account — only a one-way scrambled code of your Instagram username, so each account gets one free card.

Questions

Is this an official MBTI assessment?

No. MBTI is a trademark of The Myers-Briggs Company and this is not their instrument. PsycheAI gives a type-style reading inferred from behaviour, alongside the Big Five, which is the model most personality research uses.

How accurate is a type read from social media?

It is an inference, not a measurement, and it says how sure it is: each letter carries its strength and the card a confidence score. Thin or very private accounts give less to go on.

Can I compare my type with someone else?

Yes. Open a friend’s Psyche Card link, or scan the QR code on their card, for a free Psyche Sync: how in sync you are as friends.
