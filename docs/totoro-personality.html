What a Totoro reading means

A Totoro reading is someone with a calm, grounding presence. They like slow days, nature and small comforts, and people feel at ease around them. They show up quietly when it matters most.

You might be most like Totoro if…

  • Your saves are full of nature, cosy places and calm.
  • You post rarely and gently.
  • Your activity follows an unhurried rhythm.
  • Friends describe you as easy to be with.
