# Most like Sherlock Holmes

Observant, analytical and happiest with a puzzle to solve.

## What a Sherlock Holmes reading means

A Sherlock Holmes reading is someone who notices what other people miss and cannot leave a question alone. They go deep rather than wide, trust evidence over opinion and keep a small circle they would do anything for. Their focus is a gift they are learning to switch off now and then.

## You might be most like Sherlock Holmes if…

- You post rarely, and what you save is detailed and specific.
- You fall down research rabbit holes late at night.
- You ask precise questions in your messages.
- You keep a small, trusted circle.

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Sharp and observant
- Thorough
- Fiercely loyal to their few

### Worth watching

- Saying out loud what they are thinking
- Letting a question rest when it can
