How it works

  1. Each of you gets a Psyche Card from your own Instagram data. The first one is free.
  2. One of you sends the other their link, or they scan the QR code on your card.
  3. Once their card is ready, one tap shows how in sync you are. Psyche Sync is free.

What Psyche Sync tells you

  • Your Psyche Sync score, as a percentage, where 50% is two people picked at random, and the verdict in a few sentences.
  • What works and what to look out for between you, each with the evidence behind it.
  • How to be better friends to each other: things each of you could do, addressed to each person, and things to do together.
  • What you share: the interests, values and habits you have in common.

It is about friendship only, never romance. Download it as a PDF, or share the result as an image.

Why not just compare MBTI types?

Two people with the same four letters can be great friends or drive each other mad. What decides it is the detail: whether your energy and appetite for contact match, whether your conscientiousness is far apart (one of the most reliable sources of friction there is), whether you both argue or one always gives way, whether your rhythms can share a weekend. Psyche Sync scores those things directly, from behaviour.

Questions

Is Psyche Sync free?

Yes. Syncing two Psyche Cards is free; each person needs their own card, and the first card is free too.

Does my friend see my data?

No. A Psyche Card’s link carries only the card’s short summary, never your data export, messages or report.

Do I see my friend’s result?

Not unless they send it to you. The sync runs on the device of whoever opened the link, and stays there.

Is it a prediction about our friendship?

No. It is a reading of how two people are likely to get on, with its evidence, to start a conversation, not to decide one for you.
