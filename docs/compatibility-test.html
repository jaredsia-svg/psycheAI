How it works

  1. Each of you gets a Psyche Card from your own Instagram data. The first one is free.
  2. One of you sends the other their link. Opening it runs the compatibility analysis.
  3. Choose the basis: romantic, family / friends or professional (and, for work, who reports to whom).
  4. Get the report in under a minute. Comparisons are free.

What the report tells you

  • One honest score out of 100, where 50 is two people picked at random, and the verdict in a few sentences.
  • The best thing and the biggest risk about the pair, side by side.
  • Five dimensions chosen for the basis you picked, each scored with its reasoning and evidence. For a couple that means things like emotional safety and how you each give care; for friends, energy and appetite for contact; for work, standards, deadlines and who ends up carrying whom.
  • What works and what will rub, named plainly.
  • What each of you should do differently, addressed to each person, and things to actually talk about.

Why not just compare MBTI types?

Two people with the same four letters can be a great pair or a hopeless one. What decides it is the detail: whether your conscientiousness is far apart (the most reliable predictor of friction there is), whether you both argue or one always gives way, whether your rhythms can share a week. PsycheAI scores those things directly, from behaviour, for the one relationship you asked about.

Questions

Is the compatibility test free?

Yes. Comparing two Psyche Cards is free; each person needs their own card, and the first card is free too.

Does the other person see my data?

No. A Psyche Card’s link carries only the card’s short summary, never your data export, messages or report.

Can I compare with a colleague or my boss?

Yes. Choose professional, then whether you are peers, their manager or their report. The advice is written for that relationship and stays even-handed.

Is it a prediction about whether we will last?

No. It is a reading of how two people are likely to get on, with its evidence, to start a conversation, not to decide one for you.
