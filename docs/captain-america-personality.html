What a Captain America reading means

A Captain America reading is someone guided by clear values and a sense of duty to others. They keep their word, stand up for people and stay steady when things get difficult. They lead by example rather than by noise.

You might be most like Captain America if…

  • You keep commitments and turn up on time.
  • Your posts reflect values and causes you care about.
  • You are loyal to long-standing friends.
  • You step in when someone is treated unfairly.
