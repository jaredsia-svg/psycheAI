What a Batman reading means

A Batman reading is someone who thinks ahead, works hard and keeps a lot to themselves. They plan for every possibility and hold themselves to a demanding standard. Their commitment to the people they care about is total, even if they rarely say so.

You might be most like Batman if…

  • You post very little and keep your circle small.
  • Your activity is late at night and focused.
  • You research things thoroughly before acting.
  • You show care through actions, not words.
