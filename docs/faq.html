The basics

What is PsycheAI?

A reading of yourself already sitting in your Instagram (and Google) data. Your free summary card names the character you are most like, your signature patterns, what motivates you, your MBTI, Big Five, values, interests and love languages. The Psyche Report explains every part of it with evidence from your data. It is not a test and not a diagnosis.

How long does it take?

Instagram often takes a few hours to prepare the data zip file. The analysis takes a few minutes. Request your data first and come back when the email arrives.

What file do I need?

Your Instagram data as a .zip, in JSON format (not HTML). See the illustration for every step.

Your data

What data leaves this device?

Your Instagram zip file, your photos and videos, and your finished report never leave this device.

The only thing sent is a short digest of your own activity, usually about 100 KB of text: your posts, stories, captions, likes, searches, saves, follows and messages. You see it and can review it before anything goes.

The digest goes to Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude for that one request, and PsycheAI keeps no copy of the zip, the digest or the report on its servers. Your report stays in your browser until you press Delete everything.

Our hosting and payment providers can see that the site was used or that a payment was made, but they never receive your data. There is no account to create.

Can the digest be linked back to me?

The digest is de-identified on your device before it is sent. Your name and username become a blank, and email addresses, phone numbers, ID, card and postal numbers are removed. Everyone else’s @handles become numbers like [P1], links are dropped, and dates are cut to the month. There is no account, login or email address attached to the request, and PsycheAI keeps no copy of it.

Can anyone else access my data?

No. There is no sign-up, no password to create and no email address. Your data, the digest and the report are never stored by PsycheAI, so there is no collection of them for anyone to take.

Not even PsycheAI’s administrator can see your data or your results: there is no admin view of them, and nothing from your digest or report is stored or logged.

PsycheAI keeps only a few small things, and none of them names you: the daily totals below, a scrambled code that lets each Instagram account have one free card, simple counts for your link (such as how many people made a card from it), the short summary your link shows, and any Psyche Sync someone sends you, deleted once your device has it.

Your card summary and any shared Psyche Sync are encrypted on your device, and the key lives only in your link. PsycheAI never has the key, so no one at PsycheAI can read them.

Does PsycheAI count anything?

Only totals for each day: how many Psyche Cards, Psyche Reports and Psyche Syncs were made, how many cards followed a friend’s link, how many promo codes were used, and – for an address a creator shares (its ?via= part, which this device keeps for 14 days) – how many people arrived on it and how far they got (data loaded, card made, Psyche Report). Other visits are not counted. Nothing in those totals identifies you, your device or anything you uploaded.

Can I verify this?

The site is open source, so you can read what the browser sends. You can also open the review screen and untick anything before it goes.

The report

How accurate is it?

It is a large language model reading traces and patterns in your data, not a lab result. You can raise the report’s confidence by adding more sources. Instagram and Google together are the ideal combination – Instagram reads your outward persona, the self you present; Google reads your inward self, what you search and watch when no one is looking.

What does it cost?

Your first Psyche Card is free, and so is every Psyche Sync. Running your Psyche Card again is US$2. The Psyche Report – every section explained, plus your wellbeing, attachment style, ideal partner, career coaching and development plan – is a single US$5. You see the price before you pay.

How does Psyche Sync work?

You get one personal link, also printed on your Psyche Card as a QR code. Send it to a connection: they see your Psyche Card and can make their own for free. Then one tap shows your Psyche Sync – what clicks between you, where you differ, and how to relate better to each other. It is free, for friends, family or anyone close, never romance. If they choose to share it, the result reaches you too, with their Psyche Card: their device locks it with your link’s key, so PsycheAI cannot read it, and it is deleted once your device has it, or after 30 days.

Your link carries only your card’s short summary, never your data, messages or report, and PsycheAI cannot read it. Every 3 people who make a card from your link, or 2 who buy the full report, earn you a free Psyche Report to use or gift.
