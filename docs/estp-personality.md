# ESTP: The bold doer

ESTPs are energetic, practical and drawn to action. They read situations quickly and enjoy a challenge.

## The four letters

- **E – Extraversion**: energised by people and activity
- **S – Sensing**: focused on the concrete and practical
- **T – Thinking**: decides with logic and consistency first
- **P – Perceiving**: likes open options and adapting as they go

## How ESTP shows up in your data

- Posts from events, trips and activities
- Fast replies and lively chats
- Saves about sport, deals and adventures

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Action-oriented
- Adaptable
- Confident

### Worth watching

- Thinking long-term
- Slowing down for others
