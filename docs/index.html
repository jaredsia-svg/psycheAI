How do I get my Instagram data?

  1. In the Instagram app, open Settings → Accounts Centre.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  3. Choose Download or transfer information, pick your account, then Some of your information or All available information.
  4. Set Format to JSON — this one matters. PsycheAI cannot read the HTML format.
  5. Set Media quality to Lower quality to save space on your device.
  6. Set the date range to All time for the best results, then submit.
  7. Instagram emails you a download link, usually within a few hours. Download it to your device and upload it below.

How PsycheAI works

🔒

Your data stays with you

The .zip is opened here, in this browser. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Never leaves your device

  • The .zip itself
  • Every video in it
  • Your full written report
  • The other person's side of every DM

Sent off to be read

  • A summary: your counts, your timings, a sample of your own words
  • About 14 of your own photos, shrunk down — switch it off to send none
  • A short version of your profile, when someone scans your code

Nothing is kept on the server. Your profile lives in this browser until you press delete.

📄

What you get back

One long report, written to you, with the evidence for every claim in it.

You in one word

A noun — an animal, a material, a place — and why that one.

Big Five

Five scores out of 100, each with what in your data put it there.

MBTI

Your type, how strongly each letter leans, and what it looks like in your week.

Values & beliefs

What you seem to hold to — and how firmly the data actually says so.

In relationships

How you'd be to be close to, your attachment style, and how to love you.

At work

Where you'd thrive, how you work, and what tends to hold you back.

Your Instagram habits

When you post, how that changed, and where your attention goes.

A confidence score

How much of the above to believe, and why.

Save the whole thing as a PDF from the button on your profile.

💞

Your code, and matching

No account, no database, nothing to look up.

Your profile is squeezed down to a handful of short phrases and packed inside the QR code itself. That is the whole thing — the code is not a link to a profile, it is the profile.

When someone scans you, their phone reads your card, sends it along with theirs, and gets back two scores: romantic and platonic, kept separate because they are genuinely different questions. You can be a great friendship and a poor romance, and the report will say so. Each comes with what works, what will rub, and specific advice for each of you about the other.

⚖️

The honest bit

This is a mirror and a conversation starter. It is not a test.

Not a psychometric instrument, not a diagnosis, not a background check. It is a language model reading traces of your behaviour, and it is often wrong — which is why it tells you how sure it is. A low match score is a list of things worth talking about, not a reason to walk away. A high one is not a promise.

What it is told never to do

  • Say anything about other people in your data or your photos — they never agreed to this
  • Guess at your sexuality, health, immigration status or politics unless you said it yourself
  • Judge anyone by their appearance, or by who they follow
  • Treat a handful of photos as proof — Instagram is where people post their best day of the month
