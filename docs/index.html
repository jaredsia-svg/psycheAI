How do I get my Instagram data?

  1. In the Instagram app, open Settings → Accounts Centre.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  3. Choose Download or transfer information, pick your account, then Some of your information or All available information.
  4. Set Format to JSON — this one matters. PsycheAI cannot read the HTML format.
  5. Set Media quality to Lower quality to save space on your device.
  6. Set the date range to All time for the best results, then submit.
  7. Instagram emails you a download link, usually within a few hours. Come back with the .zip.

How PsycheAI works

What happens to your data

Your Instagram export is a .zip containing your posts, messages, contacts and location history. PsycheAI opens it in your browser using the File API and reads the JSON files describing your activity. Your videos are never opened at all.

Photographs are the one exception, and they are opt-out rather than hidden. Left switched on, PsycheAI picks roughly fourteen of your own stills — spread across your whole account history rather than the latest few — shrinks them in your browser and sends those alongside the summary. Without them the model reads only text, which is a real blind spot for anyone who posts without captions. Untick the box before uploading and not a single pixel leaves your device.

It then builds an evidence digest: your counts and activity rhythm, a sample of your own captions and comments, the accounts you follow, and the topics Instagram itself has inferred about you. That digest — not the archive — is what gets sent to the model for analysis. Direct messages are included by default because how you write to people who know you is the most revealing text in the export — but only your own messages are ever sampled, never the other side of a conversation, and you can untick that box before uploading to leave them out entirely.

Nothing is stored on the server. Your profile and reports live in this browser's local storage and are deleted when you press the delete button.

What the model is asked to do

The model is given the digest and asked to weigh it honestly: a person's own words are the strongest signal, Instagram's inferred topics are real but noisy, and accounts followed mix interest with aspiration. It is told that most people are near the middle on most traits, and to reserve extreme scores for extreme evidence.

It opens by naming you in a single noun — an animal, a material, a place, a trade — with the reasoning for why that one and not a neighbouring one. Then your Big Five with per-trait evidence; a long-form MBTI reading — the type and its nickname, how strongly each axis leans, what that letter looks like in your actual week, and a portrait written to you; your interests, values and beliefs; your strengths and weaknesses from both a relationship and a career perspective; and a read of your Instagram behaviour itself — what you post, when you are here, how it has changed, whether you publish more than you read, and where your attention goes. Plus a confidence score for the whole thing.

The finished report exports to PDF from the buttons at the top and bottom of your profile. That goes through your browser's own print dialog — choose Save as PDF — which keeps the text selectable and searchable rather than turning it into a picture.

What it is told not to do: identify or speculate about other people in your data, or infer sexual orientation, health, immigration status or political affiliation unless you have stated it outright in your own words. It does not classify anyone by appearance or by the demographics of who they follow.

The photographs carry stricter limits still. Other people appear in them and never agreed to any of this, so the model is told to say nothing at all about them — and nothing about anyone's race, body, age, attractiveness or wealth, including yours. It may read the setting, the activity, whether you are alone or in company, and the care taken over a shot. It is also told that fourteen pictures out of thousands are weak evidence, and that Instagram is where people post their best day of the month.

The QR code

Along with your full report, the model produces a compact card — the same profile reduced to short phrases. That card is deflate-compressed and encoded directly into your QR code, so there is nothing to look up and no account to create.

When someone scans you, their browser decodes your card and sends both cards to the model, which writes the compatibility report. Your long-form report never leaves your device.

How compatibility is judged

Romantic and platonic fit are scored separately, because they are different questions. Romance turns on life direction, values, emotional safety and whether two daily rhythms can coexist. Friendship turns on shared interests, compatible energy and low friction. A pair can be a great friendship and a poor romance — the report says so when that is the case.

Each mode gets a score, an honest verdict, what works, what will rub, and a playbook aimed at each person individually about the other.

Running it yourself

PsycheAI needs a server because an API key cannot ship inside a web page. Set either GEMINI_API_KEY or ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and run npm start — whichever key is present decides the provider, and PSYCHEAI_PROVIDER forces one if you have both. Set PSYCHEAI_MOCK=1 to click through the whole app with canned analyses and no API calls.

What this is not

Not a validated psychometric instrument, not a diagnosis, not a background check. A language model reading behavioural traces is a mirror and a conversation starter. A low compatibility score is a list of things worth talking about, not a reason to walk away — and a high one is not a promise.
