How do I get my Instagram data?

  1. Open Download your information. If that link does not open it, go the long way: Instagram → Settings → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions → Export / Download your information.
  2. Choose Create Export → Choose your Instagram account.
  3. Set Date Range to All time for the best results.
  4. Set Format to JSON, not HTML.
  5. Set Media Quality to lower quality to save space on your device. Start export.
  6. Instagram emails you a download link, usually within a few hours. Download it to your device and upload it below.
Optional: also add Google or Facebook data After your Instagram file is read you will be offered the chance to add either of these.

Google Takeout

  1. Open Takeout with only My Activity selected — this skips deselecting everything else and hunting for it in the list. If that link does not work, go to takeout.google.com, press Deselect all, then scroll to the middle and tick My Activity on its own.
  2. In the My Activity row, open Multiple formats and change Activity records to JSON. Takeout ships HTML by default, and an HTML export cannot be read at all.
  3. Click Next Step, Export once, as a .zip file, and Create Export. Google emails a link, usually within minutes.

Facebook

  1. In Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions.
  2. Choose Download your information → your Facebook account.
  3. Set Date Range to All time and Format to JSON.
  4. Under what to include, posts, comments, friends and messages are the parts that are read.

How PsycheAI works

🔒

Where does my data go?

The file Instagram sends you is opened and read on your own device. It is never uploaded.

Your data stays with you

After you drop your Instagram data onto the PsycheAI website, your device will summarize the contents locally to a ~100kb file, which you can review the contents of, before sending it off for analysis. The summary file is sent to Gemini or Claude for analysis and is only held for the few seconds the request takes, and is never saved, stored or logged.

You do not have to take our word for any of this. The whole thing is open source: read the code on GitHub and check what it sends for yourself.

Never leaves this device

  • The file Instagram sends you
  • All of your videos
  • Every one of your photos — none are sent, ever
  • What other people said to you in messages — counted, then discarded unread
  • Your finished report

Sent to be read by AI model

  • A short summary: how often you post, when you post, and some of your own words
  • The same kind of summary of your Google or Facebook data, if you chose to add either — never the export itself
🛡️

Can anyone else access my data?

There is no account and no list of users, so there is nothing for anyone to steal.

There is nothing to steal. PsycheAI does not have a database. There is no sign-up, no password to create, no email address, and no stored files. Nothing you send is retained, so there is no accumulated data for anyone to take.

No analytics, no trackers, no cookies. No one can see that you visited, let alone what you uploaded. There is no visitor count, no session recording, and nothing here calls out to a third party to report that this page was even opened.

Your report belongs to you. It is stored inside your own browser, on your own device. Other websites cannot read it, and neither can we — it is put together on your device from the AI's reply, and never sent back to us. It stays there until you press Delete everything, or clear your browser.

Your QR code is not a link. The short version of your profile is contained within the code itself. It does not refer to a file held anywhere else, so there is nothing behind it to break into.

What this page will not promise you

Your summary is read by Gemini or Claude through paid API access, and once it reaches them their terms apply. Both providers' terms for paid API use state that customer inputs are excluded from model training, but that is their policy to keep, not ours to guarantee.

How does compatibility testing work?

Scan the QR code of your partner, family / friends, or colleagues.

Your profile is squeezed down to a handful of short phrases and packed inside the QR code itself. That is the whole thing — the code is not a link to a profile, it is the profile. Your long report is not in there and never travels with it.

When someone scans you, they first choose what they are asking: romantic, family / friends, or professional. Those are genuinely different questions — you can be a great colleague and a poor housemate — so the report answers the one that was picked, in full, rather than three at once. Pick professional and it asks one more: whether you are colleagues, whether you manage them, or whether you report to them. Managing someone and working for them are not the same problem, and advice about delegation is useless to somebody with nobody to delegate to. It comes back with a score, five scored dimensions chosen for what you asked, an honest verdict, what works, what will rub, and specific advice for each of you about the other. Scan again to ask a different question.

Sharing your code is a choice you make one person at a time. Anyone you give it to keeps what is in it, so treat it the way you would a photo of yourself.

⚖️

What else should I know?

This is a mirror and a conversation starter. It is not a test.

Not a psychometric instrument, not a diagnosis, not a background check. It is a language model reading traces of your behaviour, and it is often wrong — which is why it tells you how sure it is. A low match score is a list of things worth talking about, not a reason to walk away. A high one is not a promise.

What it is told never to do

  • Say anything about other people in your data — they never agreed to this
  • Guess at your sexuality, health, immigration status or politics unless you said it yourself
  • Judge anyone by their appearance, or by who they follow
  • Treat one caption as a pattern — it says how many there were, so you can judge for yourself

How do I get my Instagram data?

  1. 1Open Download your information

    That link goes straight to the right page. If it does not open, go the long way inside the Instagram app — the three screens below.

  2. 2Create export, then pick your Instagram profile

    Choose the profile with the Instagram badge — not Facebook, Threads or WhatsApp.

  3. 3Change three settings, then start the export

    All three are rows on the one Confirm your export screen — ringed below. Tap each, set it, come back.

    • Date range → All time — the whole history, not a recent slice
    • Format → JSON — not HTML. An HTML export cannot be read at all, and you only find out hours later
    • Media quality → Lower quality — your photos are never read, so full resolution is gigabytes for nothing

    Then tap Start export.

  4. 4Wait for the email, then upload the file

    Download the .zip to this device and upload it at the bottom of this page.
