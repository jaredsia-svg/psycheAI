How do I get my Instagram data?
  1. In the Instagram app, open Settings → Accounts Centre.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  3. Choose Download or transfer information, pick your account, then Some of your information or All available information.
  4. Set Format to JSON — this one matters. Kindred cannot read the HTML format.
  5. Set the date range to All time for the best results, then submit.
  6. Instagram emails you a download link, usually within a few hours. Come back with the .zip.

Media quality does not matter — Kindred never opens your photos or videos, only the JSON files describing your activity.

Kindred is a self-knowledge and conversation tool, not a psychometric instrument and not a background check. It infers from behaviour, it is often wrong, and it tells you how confident it is.

How Kindred works

Where the profile comes from

Two sources, in this order.

Your Instagram export. Kindred reads the JSON files describing your posts and captions, stories and reels, comments you have written, posts you liked and saved, the accounts you follow, the topics Instagram itself has inferred about you, your bio, and — only if you opt in — aggregate statistics about your direct messages. It never opens your photos.

The questionnaire. A three-step personality test covering background, personality and relationship preferences. Every answer that overlaps with something Kindred guessed overrides the guess. The Instagram analysis is a starting point, not a verdict.

How the personality estimate is made

Big Five traits are estimated from a weighted blend of language markers and behaviour: the words you use, how varied your vocabulary is, how regularly you post, what time of day you are active, how many people you engage with, and which themes dominate your activity. This follows the open-vocabulary approach used in personality research — language use does correlate with personality, but modestly, and only in aggregate.

So every trait shows you the evidence that moved it, and the whole profile carries a confidence figure driven by how much data your export actually contained. A thin export produces a hedged profile, and says so.

What Kindred does not infer: your attachment style, your love languages, your dealbreakers, your religion or whether you want children. Nothing in an Instagram export predicts these reliably, so they come from your answers only. Kindred also does not classify the people you follow by appearance or gender.

How compatibility is scored

Eight dimensions, weighted differently for romance and friendship — because what makes a good partner is not what makes a good friend. Shared interests carry a friendship; attachment style and life direction decide a relationship.

Dealbreakers are only ever checked against habits the other person has declared about themselves. Anything that cannot be checked — infidelity, anger, drugs — is listed as a conversation to have rather than folded into a score.

Privacy

There is no server, no account and no database. The page is static; your export is read in the browser with the File API, and your profile is kept in this browser's local storage. Your QR code contains only structured fields — your name, background, tick-box answers, trait scores and a digest of your Instagram signals. Your written explanations are never encoded into it.

Deleting your profile on the profile page removes everything Kindred has stored.

What this is not

Kindred is not a validated psychometric instrument, not a diagnosis, and not a background check. It is a mirror and a conversation starter. A low score is a list of things worth talking about, not a reason to walk away — and a high score is not a promise.
