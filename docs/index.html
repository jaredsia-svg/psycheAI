        The basics

        What is PsycheAI?

        A reading of yourself already sitting in your Instagram (and Google) data. Your free summary card names the character you are most like, your signature patterns, what motivates you, your MBTI, Big Five, values, interests and love languages. The full premium report explains every part of it with evidence from your data. It is not a test and not a diagnosis.

        How long does it take?

        Instagram often takes a few hours to prepare the data zip file. The analysis takes a few minutes. Request the export first and come back when the email arrives.

        What file do I need?

        Your Instagram export as a .zip, in JSON format (not HTML). See the illustration for every step.

        🔒

        Your data

        What data leaves this device?

        Never sent: the Instagram zip data, your media (images / videos), the finished report, and other people’s messages — apart from a short, anonymised line of what some of your replies were answering.

        Sent, after you review it: a short digest of your words — posts, stories, captions, likes, searches, saves, follows, your messages.

        The digest goes to Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude for that one request. PsycheAI keeps no copy of the zip, the digest or the report on its servers. The report lives in your browser until you press Delete everything.

        Hosting and payment providers can see that the site was called, or that a payment happened. They do not receive the data. There is no account.

        Can the digest be linked back to me?

        The digest is de-identified on your device before it is sent. Your name and username become a blank, and email addresses, phone numbers, ID, card and postal numbers are removed. Everyone else’s @handles become numbers like [P1], links are dropped, and dates are cut to the month. There is no account, login or email address attached to the request, and PsycheAI keeps no copy of it.

        What remains is your own writing and the accounts you engage with most, because that is what the reading is made from. So it is de-identified rather than anonymous: a stranger holding it would not know whose it is, but someone who already knows you well might recognise you in it.

        Can anyone else access my data?

        There is no sign-up, no password to create and no email address. Your export, the digest and the report are never stored by PsycheAI, so there is no collection of them for anyone to take.

        What PsycheAI does keep, in a small database (Upstash): the daily totals below; a one-way scrambled code of your Instagram username, so each account gets one free card; for your link, how many times it was opened and how many friends made a card or bought the full report from it (plain numbers, never who); and, once you share your link, a locked copy of your card’s short summary — locked on your device with a key that is only in the link itself, so PsycheAI holds it but cannot read it. None of it names you.

        Does PsycheAI count anything?

        Only totals for each day: how many Psyche Cards, full reports and Psyche Syncs were made, how many cards followed a friend’s link, how many promo codes were used, and — for an address a creator shares (its ?via= part, which this device keeps for 14 days) — how many people arrived on it and how far they got (export loaded, card made, full report). Other visits are not counted. Nothing in those totals identifies you, your device or anything you uploaded.

        Why is there one free card per Instagram account?

        To keep the free card free for everyone. Your browser turns your Instagram username into a one-way scrambled code and sends only that; PsycheAI scrambles it once more and keeps the result, to know the account has had its free card. It cannot be turned back into your username, and it is not linked to anything you uploaded. If you invite friends, the same kind of code is how a friend is counted once.

        Can I verify this?

        The site is open source, so you can read what the browser sends. You can also open the review screen and untick anything before it goes.

        The report

        How accurate is it?

        It is a large language model reading traces and patterns in your data, not a lab result. You can raise the report’s confidence by adding more sources. Instagram and Google together are the ideal combination — Instagram reads your outward persona, the self you present; Google reads your inward self, what you search and watch when no one is looking.

        What does it cost?

        Your first Psyche Card is free, and so is every Psyche Sync. Running your Psyche Card again is US$2. The full premium report — every section explained, plus your wellbeing, attachment style, ideal partner, career coaching, development plan and a secret bonus section — is a single US$5. You see the price before you pay.

        How does Psyche Sync work?

        Each reader has one short personal link, like psycheai.io/c/k7Qm2xPa1Z#…, also on their card as a QR code. Send it to a friend and they see your Psyche Card — the same card you can post as an image — with a way to make their own. Once they have, one tap shows how in sync the two of you are as friends: a Psyche Sync score and how to relate better to each other, free. It is about friendship only, never romance. The result stays on their device: you do not see it unless they send it to you. It also counts towards a free full report for you: every 3 friends who make a card from your link, or 2 who buy the full report, earn one, which you can use or gift to someone. My Psyche shows how many friends made a card from your link, bought the full report and synced with you. The link points to a locked copy of your card’s short summary, never your export, messages or report. The key that opens it is the part after the #, which a browser never sends to a server, so PsycheAI stores the locked copy but cannot read it. The link stays the same if your card is redrawn, and shows the new one.
