      FAQ

      📖

      The basics

      What is PsycheAI?

      A reading of yourself already sitting in your Instagram (and Google) data. Your free summary card names the character you are most like, your signature patterns, what motivates you, your MBTI, Big Five, values, interests and love languages. The full premium report explains every part of it with evidence from your data. It is not a test and not a diagnosis.

      How long does it take?

      Instagram often takes a few hours to prepare the data zip file. The analysis takes a few minutes. Request the export first and come back when the email arrives.

      What file do I need?

      Your Instagram export as a .zip, in JSON format (not HTML). See the illustration for every step.

      🔒

      Your data

      What data leaves this device?

      Never sent: the Instagram zip data, your media (images / videos), other people’s messages, the finished report.

      Sent, after you review it: a short digest of your words — posts, stories, captions, likes, searches, saves, follows, your messages.

      The digest goes to Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude for that one request. PsycheAI keeps no copy of the zip, the digest or the report on its servers. The report lives in your browser until you press Delete everything.

      Hosting and payment providers can see that the site was called, or that a payment happened. They do not receive the data. There is no account.

      Can the digest be linked back to me?

      The digest is de-identified on your device before it is sent. Your name and username become a blank, and email addresses, phone numbers, ID, card and postal numbers are removed. Everyone else’s @handles become numbers like [P1], links are dropped, and dates are cut to the month. There is no account, login or email address attached to the request, and PsycheAI keeps no copy of it.

      What remains is your own writing and the accounts you engage with most, because that is what the reading is made from. So it is de-identified rather than anonymous: a stranger holding it would not know whose it is, but someone who already knows you well might recognise you in it.

      Can anyone else access my data?

      PsycheAI has no database. There is no sign-up, no password to create, no email address and no stored files. Nothing you send is retained, so there is no accumulated data for anyone to take. No one can see that you visited, let alone what you uploaded.

      Does PsycheAI count anything?

      Only totals for each day: how many Psyche Cards, full reports and compatibility reports were made, how many cards followed a friend’s compatibility link, how many came from a campaign link (the ?via= part of an address a creator shares, which this device keeps for 14 days), and how many times each promo code was used. Visits are not counted. Nothing in those totals identifies you, your device or anything you uploaded.

      Can I verify this?

      The site is open source, so you can read what the browser sends. You can also open the review screen and untick anything before it goes.

      📄

      The report

      How accurate is it?

      It is a large language model reading traces and patterns in your data, not a lab result. You can raise the report’s confidence by adding more sources. Instagram and Google together are the ideal combination — Instagram reads your outward persona, the self you present; Google reads your inward self, what you search and watch when no one is looking.

      What does it cost?

      Your first Psyche Card is free, and so is every compatibility report. Running your Psyche Card again is US$2. The full premium report — every section explained, plus your wellbeing, attachment style, ideal partner, career coaching, development plan and a secret bonus section — is a single US$5. You see the price before you pay.

      How does the compatibility feature work?

      Each reader has a personal link that carries a summary of their PsycheAI results. Send it to someone; once they open it and make their own Psyche Card, they choose a romantic, family/friends or work basis and get a report, free. The link carries the data itself, in the part after the # that a browser never sends to a server — it is not a link to a file on a server.
