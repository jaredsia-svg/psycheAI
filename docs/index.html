How do I get my Instagram data?
  1. In the Instagram app, open Settings → Accounts Centre.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  3. Choose Download or transfer information, pick your account, then Some of your information or All available information.
  4. Set Format to JSON — this one matters. Kindred cannot read the HTML format.
  5. Set the date range to All time for the best results, then submit.
  6. Instagram emails you a download link, usually within a few hours. Come back with the .zip.

Media quality does not matter — Kindred never opens your photos or videos, only the JSON files describing your activity.

Kindred is a self-knowledge and conversation tool, not a psychometric instrument and not a background check. The model is inferring from behaviour, it is often wrong, and it says how confident it is.

How Kindred works

What happens to your data

Your Instagram export is a .zip containing your posts, messages, contacts and location history. Kindred opens it in your browser using the File API and reads only the JSON files describing your activity — never your photos or videos.

It then builds an evidence digest: your counts and activity rhythm, a sample of your own captions and comments, the accounts you follow, and the topics Instagram itself has inferred about you. That digest — not the archive — is what gets sent to the model for analysis. Direct messages are excluded unless you opt in, and even then only your own messages are sampled.

Nothing is stored on the server. Your profile and reports live in this browser's local storage and are deleted when you press the delete button.

What the model is asked to do

The model is given the digest and asked to weigh it honestly: a person's own words are the strongest signal, Instagram's inferred topics are real but noisy, and accounts followed mix interest with aspiration. It is told that most people are near the middle on most traits, and to reserve extreme scores for extreme evidence.

It produces your Big Five with per-trait evidence, an MBTI guess with per-letter reasoning and a caveat, your interests, beliefs and values, and your strengths and weaknesses from both a relationship and a career perspective — plus a confidence score for the whole thing.

What it is told not to do: identify or speculate about other people in your data, or infer sexual orientation, health, immigration status or political affiliation unless you have stated it outright in your own words. It does not classify anyone by appearance or by the demographics of who they follow.

The QR code

Along with your full report, the model produces a compact card — the same profile reduced to short phrases. That card is deflate-compressed and encoded directly into your QR code, so there is nothing to look up and no account to create.

When someone scans you, their browser decodes your card and sends both cards to the model, which writes the compatibility report. Your long-form report never leaves your device.

How compatibility is judged

Romantic and platonic fit are scored separately, because they are different questions. Romance turns on life direction, values, emotional safety and whether two daily rhythms can coexist. Friendship turns on shared interests, compatible energy and low friction. A pair can be a great friendship and a poor romance — the report says so when that is the case.

Each mode gets a score, an honest verdict, what works, what will rub, and a playbook aimed at each person individually about the other.

Running it yourself

Kindred needs a server because an API key cannot ship inside a web page. Set either GEMINI_API_KEY or ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and run npm start — whichever key is present decides the provider, and KINDRED_PROVIDER forces one if you have both. Set KINDRED_MOCK=1 to click through the whole app with canned analyses and no API calls.

What this is not

Not a validated psychometric instrument, not a diagnosis, not a background check. A language model reading behavioural traces is a mirror and a conversation starter. A low compatibility score is a list of things worth talking about, not a reason to walk away — and a high one is not a promise.
