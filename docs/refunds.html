Refund policy

Changed your mind? Ask within 24 hours of paying for a full refund — no reason needed.

PsycheAI · Singapore · Last updated 9 October 2026

What can be refunded

  • The full premium report and the summary card re-run: a full refund if you ask within 24 hours of payment.
  • After 24 hours, payments are not refundable, except where the law requires it or the report was never delivered (below).
  • Compatibility reports are free, as are reports unlocked with a promo code or by inviting friends, so there is nothing to refund.

If a report fails

If a paid report fails to generate, try again from the same device: your payment covers the retry, and you will not be charged twice. If it still cannot be delivered, we will refund it at any time.

How to ask

Email admin@psycheai.io with the date and amount of the payment and the last four digits of the card (or the wallet) you paid with. There is no account, so these are how we find your payment.

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank — it is quicker for you.
