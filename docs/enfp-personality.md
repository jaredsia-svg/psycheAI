# ENFP: The enthusiastic explorer

ENFPs are curious, imaginative and energised by people and possibilities. They bring warmth and spark to everything they start.

## The four letters

- **E – Extraversion**: energised by people and activity
- **N – Intuition**: drawn to ideas, patterns and possibilities
- **F – Feeling**: decides with people and values first
- **P – Perceiving**: likes open options and adapting as they go

## How ENFP shows up in your data

- Bursts of posting around new interests
- Lots of warm, chatty messages
- Saves full of ideas, places and plans

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Enthusiastic
- Creative
- Warm and encouraging

### Worth watching

- Seeing plans through
- Pacing their energy
