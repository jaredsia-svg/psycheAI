# A sample PsycheAI report

This is the reading for **Emily Carter**, a fictional reader, so you can see what yours looks like. Every finding cites the evidence in the reader’s own data.

## Most like Mulan

**Organiser with a warm centre and a quiet battery**

Like her, you step up for the people around you and see it through: four years of posts naming a plan and closing it out. When it goes well, the win is written as ours rather than mine, the same quiet competence that shares the credit. Underneath, you read as an organiser with a warm centre, always at the thing and then taking a quiet stretch to recharge. What you value shows in what you do rather than what you say, because your captions barely mention you without attaching the news to somebody else.

You do the thing nobody assigned you and you do it quietly, which is the resemblance — not the sword. Forty-seven posts naming a deadline and forty-one closing it out, four years of it, and the wins written as ours rather than mine. The comparison earns itself on what is missing: no post in four years says the load was heavy, and Mulan is the same kind of competent that would rather absorb the cost than be seen carrying it.

## Signature patterns

- **The quiet organiser**: You do the unassigned work that keeps a group running – the plan, the chasing, the remembering – which makes you indispensable and, on this evidence, under-credited.
- **Generous with attention**: You show up warmly for other people and say almost nothing about yourself, so closeness runs one way: people feel looked after by you and rarely get to look after you.
- **Bursts, then recovery**: Intense runs of hosting and posting around events you arranged, then a fortnight of quiet – the rhythm of someone who is social on purpose and recharges alone.

## Big Five

- **Openness – 61**  
Curious inside familiar territory: the same three trailheads for four years, with the books and routes within them always changing.
- **Conscientiousness – 84**  
Your strongest trait: you post plans before they happen, follow through after, and the gap between the two is small.
- **Extraversion – 55**  
Social in bursts and solitary in between: you enjoy people, then appear to need recovering from them.
- **Agreeableness – 79**  
Almost every comment you write is about somebody else's news, and on the rare occasions you disagree, you soften it first.
- **Emotional sensitivity – 44**  
Steadier than most, with one tell: your posting drops sharply for a week or two after your busiest stretches.

## MBTI: ENFJ

- **E** (slight): You make the occasion rather than attend it, for a genuinely wide circle, and you go looking for the big rooms as well as the familiar ones. Against it, you leave most of them early and twice call yourself "peopled out" the next day, which keeps this about as close as an axis gets.
- **N** (slight): Your captions reach for meaning well beyond what a caption needs to do. The other twenty-eight captions are precise description — places, dates, model names — and the last eighteen months carry a steady run of how-to searching, but Instagram rewards exactly that specificity, so it moderates the letter rather than overturning it.
- **F** (slight): When the two pull apart, the person breaks the tie, not the principle. The warm comments are not counted, since every account looks kind in a comment box. What counts against it: your forty-seven deadline posts track delivery rather than who was affected, and your captions about other people's work assess it about as often as they celebrate it, and that is the real case against it.
- **J** (moderate): You follow through on plans you set yourself, which is more than a posting habit. The nearest contrary reading is that your posting comes in bursts, but the bursts land on scheduled events, so they show planning rather than spontaneity, and the data leaves little room to argue.
