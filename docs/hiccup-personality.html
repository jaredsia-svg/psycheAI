What a Hiccup reading means

A Hiccup reading is someone thoughtful and inventive who goes their own way. They question how things are done, solve problems creatively and build bridges between people who do not understand each other. Their gentleness is a strength.

You might be most like Hiccup if…

  • You save and search for how things work.
  • You build, tinker or draw.
  • You see the other side of arguments.
  • You keep a close bond with one or two people.
