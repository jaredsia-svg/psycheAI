What a Odysseus reading means

An Odysseus reading is someone with a plan for every problem and a better one when it fails. They are inventive, persuasive and at their best when a challenge looks impossible. However far they range, they keep working their way back to the people and places they call home.

You might be most like Odysseus if…

  • Your saves and searches are full of ideas, tools and places to go.
  • You talk your way into opportunities and out of trouble.
  • You work in intense bursts on whatever has caught your interest.
  • You keep coming back to the same few people, wherever you are.

Strengths and what to watch

Strengths

  • Persuasive
  • Thinks fast under pressure

Worth watching

  • Sharing the credit with the crew
