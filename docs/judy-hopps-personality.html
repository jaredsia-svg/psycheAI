What a Judy Hopps reading means

A Judy Hopps reading is someone with a clear sense of right and a lot of energy to act on it. They set goals, work for them, and do not take no for an answer when they believe in something. They are at their best when that drive is matched with listening.

You might be most like Judy Hopps if…

  • Your activity shows routines, goals and things ticked off.
  • You post achievements and the work behind them.
  • You stand up for friends and causes in the comments.
  • You reply fast and like to get things moving.
