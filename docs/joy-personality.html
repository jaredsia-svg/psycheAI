What a Joy reading means

A Joy reading is someone who brings energy and warmth wherever they go. They plan good times, cheer people on and look for the bright side. Their greatest strength becomes even stronger when they make room for the quieter feelings too.

You might be most like Joy if…

  • Your posts are full of celebrations, friends and plans.
  • You comment encouragingly on lots of people’s posts.
  • You are first to reply in group chats.
  • You organise the fun.

Strengths and what to watch

Strengths

  • Optimistic and energising
  • Encouraging
  • Brings people together

Worth watching

  • Letting harder feelings have space
  • Slowing down sometimes
