PsycheAI in Muse: privacy

What the PsycheAI plugin for Muse receives, what it keeps, and what it never does.

PsycheAI · Singapore · Last updated 30 October 2026 · PsycheAI in Muse

Privacy

The short version. Muse reads your Instagram on your behalf. We never receive your posts, captions, comments, stories or messages.

Your read. Muse writes your Psyche read and sends it to PsycheAI once, to make your private link. It is stored encrypted under a key that exists only in your link, so we can't read it, and it is deleted after 7 days. If you use PsycheAI as a Muse skill, your report is also kept in your own Muse workspace, never on our server, so Muse can answer your questions about it in later chats; when you Ask PsycheAI something, Muse may look at your Instagram again the same way, on your own account, to answer. Its PDF is made at the same moment and locked with the same key, and the locked copy is deleted with the read. The key is only in the part of the PDF link after the #, which your browser never sends to us: the page fetches the locked PDF and unlocks it in your browser. When the skill saves the PDF into your Muse chat, it sends the key with the request, never in the address, and our server unlocks the PDF for that moment, keeping nothing. Once you open it, it lives in your browser, like any PsycheAI read. WhatsApp chats you add through the skill are made ready in your own Muse workspace first: only your own messages are kept, the other people in each chat become Friend A, Friend B and so on wherever their names appear, and numbers, emails, links and handles are taken out. Muse reads that copy, never the export, and nothing from the chats reaches PsycheAI except the report written from them; you can delete them from your workspace when it's done. A Deep Psyche keeps its notes on each year in the same place. With the skill, what Muse reads from your Instagram for a read is held in the skill's own working folder and deleted as soon as your report is saved; what stays in your workspace is the sample of your own words your report was written from (your own captions, story text, comments and your side of your messages, dated, with no one else's messages and no handles, links or numbers), so a fresh report, added chats or your questions can start from it. Saying "Delete my PsycheAI data" removes it with everything else. To draw your card image, the plugin keeps the card-level summary (character, headline, type, pattern names, top motivations, a few interests) for the same 7 days, locked under a key that is kept in your own Muse workspace and in your card picture's link, never on our server, so a copy of our database can't be read. These keys, and the sign-in for your PsycheAI account, are files in your Muse workspace, where other skills you install in the same workspace could read them: install only skills you trust. The key comes with your own requests: while one of them runs (drawing your card, for instance), our server unlocks the summary for that moment and keeps nothing readable.

If you join Psyche Pair, we keep what's needed to match you: first name, age, gender, who you'd like to meet, city, Instagram handle, the three photos you choose (made smaller in your browser before they're sent, kept encrypted, shown only to the people you're matched with, and deleted when you delete your data), your answers about religion, kids and the like, and scored traits (needs, lifestyle, future plans, communication, values, background, personality), each marked as from Instagram or from you, with a confidence level. Also your preferences, your answers to matching questions, who was shown to whom, picks and answers, and, for date check-ins, only whether you met and would again. All of it is encrypted at rest, and your email is kept apart from your profile.

If you join from psycheai.io (Use for Psyche Pair, under your read there), your browser sends the card-level part of your read: type, Big Five, attachment lean, love languages, interests, values, motivations, character, headline and pattern names. It's held for an hour while you sign in, then kept as part of your Psyche Pair profile, marked as from your read. Anything you tell Muse yourself always replaces it.

If you use Sync, we keep the card-level profile from your latest read (character, type, Big Five, pattern names, motivations, interests, values, how you show care), so friends can sync with you by your link, until you delete it. It's locked under your Sync link's 22-character code, which we never store (only a fingerprint of it, to find it). Each Sync, both cards and what Muse wrote, is locked the same way under the code it was made with, which only your workspace and your friend's hold. While you or your friend use a Sync, our server unlocks it for that moment to draw it and make its PDF, and keeps nothing readable. Never your posts, messages, report or Instagram handle: Sync works by link only. Someone you sync with sees your card-level profile through their own Muse.

If you buy Plus, Stripe takes the payment, and we never see your card. We keep your Stripe customer and subscription ids, whether it's active, and when it renews or ends.

Your email, only if you add one. Connecting needs no email: your account is made on the spot, and a small cookie lets this browser find it again. If you add an email, it's for sign-in codes and at most one digest a day when you've been away. Sign-in codes and tokens are stored only in hashed form.

What the skill sends, and from where. PsycheAI installs only from app.psycheai.io, and every update is signed: an installed skill checks the signature before installing anything, and never installs an older release over a newer one. The skill itself checks what it sends us before it leaves your workspace: a report or a Sync, no bigger than a report, with no handles, links, emails or phone numbers in it. A friend's card that reaches your Muse in a Sync is cleaned first, so it's only their card, never instructions.

What we never do. We never guess your religion, ethnicity, politics, health, drug use or height: these come only from you, and the optional ones can stay blank. We never judge looks from photos, never sell your data, and never use it for ads.

Who sees what. A match sees your card in stages: photo, first name, age, city, headline, character and fit in the weekly drop; more if you pick each other. Your Instagram shows only if you both choose to swap. Muse (Meta) sees what passes through your chat with it, under Meta's own terms.

Private chat. If you use the web chat instead of Muse, messages are encrypted when stored. Nobody at PsycheAI reads them unless one of you reports the other: a report keeps that conversation for up to 90 days for our safety review. Otherwise messages are deleted after 30 days, or straight away if either of you ends the chat or blocks the other.

Your choices. Change your profile any time by telling Muse. Pause matching, or say "Delete my PsycheAI data" to remove your card, profile, preferences, answers, matches, intros, chats and Syncs. Or email admin@psycheai.io.

Where. PsycheAI is operated from Singapore and follows the Personal Data Protection Act. Data is stored with our hosting and database providers, encrypted in transit and at rest. The rest of PsycheAI is covered by the main privacy policy.
