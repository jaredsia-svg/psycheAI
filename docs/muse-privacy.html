PsycheAI in Muse: privacy

Your data is read in Muse, on your own account, and your report and Psyche Card are made there too. None of your posts, messages or report come to us, and your Psyche Card's few lines and motivation scores only if you choose to Sync with a friend.

PsycheAI · Singapore · Last updated 4 November 2026 · PsycheAI in Muse

In short

  • 🔒 Read in your own Muse account. Your Instagram, and any WhatsApp chats, Facebook posts or Messenger messages you add, are read in your own Muse account: nothing to export or upload.
  • 🙈 De-identified first before analysis. Your posts and chats are de-identified before your Psyche is written: names, handles, numbers and links removed, and no one else is quoted.
  • 🚫 None of your data sent to PsycheAI. Your report and Psyche Card are made in your own Muse workspace. None of your posts, messages or report go to PsycheAI; only if you choose to Sync are your card’s few lines and motivation scores shared.
  • ✋ Private to you. Nothing is posted or shared with other people unless you choose to. Delete it all any time: say "Delete my PsycheAI data" in Muse, or tap My data and privacy.

What we receive

None of your posts, messages or report. The skill asks our server only for its own account (no name or email), its menus, signed updates and the art your card is drawn with. If you choose to Sync with a friend, your card's few lines come to us, locked: your character, headline, type, pattern names, top motivations, a few interests and values, your Big Five scores, your scores on the ten motivations and how you show care. A friend you Sync with sees them on your Sync's page.

What we keep

  • Your card, only if you Sync, locked. Your card's few lines and motivation scores, for 7 days, locked under a key kept in your own Muse workspace, never on our server. Our server unlocks it only while it works on your Syncs, and keeps nothing readable.
  • Sync, if you use it. The same card-level profile, so friends can sync with you by your link, locked under your Sync link's 22-character code, which we never store. Never your posts, messages, report or Instagram handle.
  • Your account. A random ID for your Muse workspace, when things happened and a few counts, with no name, email or Instagram handle.
  • A waitlist, if you join one. Only the city you give and when you joined, so we can tell you when it opens there.

What stays in your Muse

With the PsycheAI skill, your report and the de-identified sample of your own words it was written from stay in your own Muse workspace, so you can ask about it later. Chats and Facebook you add are de-identified there too. What Muse reads for a read is deleted as soon as your report is saved. Your keys are files there, where other skills you install in the same workspace could read them: install only skills you trust.

What we never do

We never guess your religion, ethnicity, politics, health, drug use or height: these come only from you. We never judge looks from photos, never sell your data, and never use it for ads.

Check it yourself

The plugin's code, the skill included, is public at github.com/jaredsia-svg/psycheai-app. It installs only from app.psycheai.io, every update is signed, and it never installs an older release over a newer one. Before anything leaves your workspace, the skill itself checks it has no handles, links, emails or phone numbers.

Who, and where

Muse (Meta) sees what passes through your chat with it, under Meta's own terms. PsycheAI is operated from Singapore under the Personal Data Protection Act; data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Questions: admin@psycheai.io. The rest of PsycheAI is covered by the main privacy policy.
