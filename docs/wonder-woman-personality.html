What a Wonder Woman reading means

A Wonder Woman reading is someone strong in conviction and generous in spirit. They stand up for others, believe in people and lead from the front. Their strength is matched by real compassion, which is what makes people follow them.

You might be most like Wonder Woman if…

  • You speak up for causes and for friends.
  • Your posts show both strength and kindness.
  • People look to you to lead.
  • You set high standards and help others meet them.
