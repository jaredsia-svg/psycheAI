# ISFP: The quiet creator

ISFPs are gentle, observant and creative. They enjoy beauty, experiences and expressing themselves in their own way.

## The four letters

- **I – Introversion**: energised by time alone and close company
- **S – Sensing**: focused on the concrete and practical
- **F – Feeling**: decides with people and values first
- **P – Perceiving**: likes open options and adapting as they go

## How ISFP shows up in your data

- Aesthetic posts and saves
- Quiet, kind messages
- Activity around art, music, nature or style

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Creative
- Kind
- Present in the moment

### Worth watching

- Planning ahead
- Speaking up for what they want
