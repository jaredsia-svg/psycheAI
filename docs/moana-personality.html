What a Moana reading means

A Moana reading is someone pulled forward by curiosity and a sense of purpose. They want to see things for themselves, take the long way if it teaches them something, and bring others along with their enthusiasm. They care about where they come from as much as where they are going.

You might be most like Moana if…

  • Your saves and searches are full of places, ideas and things to try.
  • You start conversations and keep them going.
  • You talk about family and roots alongside big plans.
  • Once you decide on something, you see it through.
