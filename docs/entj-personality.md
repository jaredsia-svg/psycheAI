# ENTJ: The decisive organiser

ENTJs set goals, organise people and drive towards results. They are confident, direct and energised by a challenge.

## The four letters

- **E – Extraversion**: energised by people and activity
- **N – Intuition**: drawn to ideas, patterns and possibilities
- **T – Thinking**: decides with logic and consistency first
- **J – Judging**: likes plans, decisions and closure

## How ENTJ shows up in your data

- Posts about milestones, work and goals
- Quick, decisive replies
- Groups they lead or coordinate

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Leadership
- Clear decisions
- Gets things done

### Worth watching

- Listening before deciding
- Leaving space for feelings in a plan
