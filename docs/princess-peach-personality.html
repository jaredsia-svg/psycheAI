What a Princess Peach reading means

A Princess Peach reading is someone who is kind, sociable and quietly competent. They look after people, keep things running smoothly and handle more than they let on. They are at their best when they take the lead they are clearly ready for.

You might be most like Princess Peach if…

  • You host, organise and make people feel welcome.
  • Your posts are warm and well put together.
  • You keep up with a wide circle.
  • You handle a lot calmly.
