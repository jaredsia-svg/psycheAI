What a Remy reading means

A Remy reading is someone with a real craft and the drive to get better at it. They care about quality, notice details others miss, and light up when they get to make or share something they love. They do their best when they let people help.

You might be most like Remy if…

  • Your saves and searches circle one passion in depth.
  • You post what you make, and talk about how you made it.
  • You have strong, well-informed opinions on your craft.
  • You light up when someone shares your interest.
