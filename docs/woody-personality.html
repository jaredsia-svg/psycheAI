What a Woody reading means

A Woody reading is someone who takes care of the group and takes the role seriously. They organise, check in, and want everyone to feel part of things. They care deeply about their people and do their best work when they share the lead.

You might be most like Woody if…

  • You run or start the group chats.
  • You plan the meet-ups and make sure people turn up.
  • Your posts are about your crew and shared history.
  • You keep friendships going for years.
