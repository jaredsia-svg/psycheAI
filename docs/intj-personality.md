# INTJ: The quiet strategist

INTJs think in systems and long plans. They work independently, value competence and like to understand why something works before trusting it.

## The four letters

- **I – Introversion**: energised by time alone and close company
- **N – Intuition**: drawn to ideas, patterns and possibilities
- **T – Thinking**: decides with logic and consistency first
- **J – Judging**: likes plans, decisions and closure

## How INTJ shows up in your data

- Few posts, mostly about projects, ideas or progress
- Searches and saves that go deep on a handful of subjects
- Short, direct messages with a small circle

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Long-range thinking
- Independent and self-driven
- High standards

### Worth watching

- Explaining the plan before acting on it
- Making room for other people’s pace
