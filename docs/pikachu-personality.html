What a Pikachu reading means

A Pikachu reading is someone bright, playful and fiercely loyal to their closest friend or friends. They are easy to be around, brave when it counts, and happiest when they are with their people. Their affection is obvious and generous.

You might be most like Pikachu if…

  • Your posts are playful and full of friends.
  • You react to friends’ posts quickly and warmly.
  • You stick by your closest friends through everything.
  • You bring energy to any plan.
