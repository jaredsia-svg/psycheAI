What a Spider-Man reading means

A Spider-Man reading is someone with a big heart, quick humour and many plates spinning. They care about their neighbourhood and their friends, want to help everyone, and keep it light even when life is busy. They thrive when they let people help with the load.

You might be most like Spider-Man if…

  • Your activity spikes late at night after busy days.
  • You joke a lot in chats.
  • You help friends with favours big and small.
  • You have a lot going on and post about some of it.
