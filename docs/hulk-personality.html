What a Hulk reading means

A Hulk reading is someone who is calm and considered day to day but feels things strongly. They are brilliant, curious and kind, and when they care about something their energy is enormous. They do best when they make room to let off steam.

You might be most like Hulk if…

  • Your saves include science, how-things-work and deep dives.
  • You are calm and measured in most chats.
  • You feel strongly about a few causes.
  • You need time out to reset.
