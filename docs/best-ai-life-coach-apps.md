# The best AI life coach apps in 2026

The short answer: almost every AI coach knows only what you tell it, in chat or a journal. Pick by how you like to work: daily goals (Rocky.ai), journaling (Rosebud, Mindsera), open conversation (Pi, ChatGPT, Claude) or a coach that challenges you (Purpose). PsycheAI is the one that starts from your actual behaviour, read from your own data.

## The list

### 1. Rocky.ai

A daily coaching chat: reflective questions, goals and check-ins that hold you to them, with a free tier. **Knows you from:** what you tell it each day. **Best for:** building a habit of reflection.

### 2. Rosebud and Mindsera

AI journals: you write, and they reflect patterns back, ask follow-up questions and summarise your entries over time. **Knows you from:** your journal. **Best for:** people who already like writing things down.

### 3. Pi, ChatGPT and Claude

General assistants, warm and capable, and good to think aloud with. With memory turned on they remember what you have said before. **Knows you from:** your conversations, and whatever you upload. **Best for:** working through one problem at a time.

### 4. Purpose

A coaching app from the author Mark Manson, built to challenge your assumptions rather than reassure you. **Knows you from:** chat and a values intake. **Best for:** people who want to be pushed.

### 5. BetterUp

Human coaches with AI support, mostly through employers. **Knows you from:** assessments and sessions. **Best for:** work and leadership, if your company offers it.

### 6. Ask PsycheAI (ours)

Starts from a full personality study of your own Instagram data (and, if you like, WhatsApp, Google or Facebook): your type, traits, patterns and values, each with its evidence. You skip the background and ask the real question, such as [am I in the wrong job?](https://psycheai.io/am-i-in-the-wrong-job) or [what will I regret when I am old?](https://psycheai.io/what-will-i-regret-when-im-old) Your first question is free. **Knows you from:** what you actually do. **Best for:** deep questions about yourself.

## Why where the advice comes from matters

A coach that knows only what you tell it can only work with the story you already believe about yourself. That is fine for goals and accountability. For the hard questions, what you are avoiding, what you will regret, why a pattern keeps repeating, the most useful evidence is often what you would not think to say. That is the gap a coach built on your own behaviour fills.

Several have free tiers: Rocky.ai for daily goals, Pi and ChatGPT for conversation. PsycheAI gives a free personality reading and one free question to its coach.

### Can an AI coach replace a therapist?

No. AI coaches are for reflection and everyday decisions. For anything affecting your health or safety, talk to someone qualified.
