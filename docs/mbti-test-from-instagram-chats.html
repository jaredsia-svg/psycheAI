What your chats show about each letter

  • E or I: how many different people you seek out, who starts the conversations and how you recover after busy stretches, not simply how much you talk.
  • S or N: whether you write about concrete plans and details, or ideas, possibilities and what things mean.
  • T or F: how you handle a disagreement or a friend’s bad news: solutions and reasons, or feelings and reassurance.
  • J or P: plans made and kept, replies that close things off, or open threads and last-minute changes.

The 16 types, and more on an MBTI test without questions.

How it works

  1. Request your Instagram data (about 30 seconds; the file usually arrives within 1–2 hours). Step-by-step guide. No time to wait? Export two or three WhatsApp chats instead.
  2. Load the files in PsycheAI. They are read in your own browser and reduced to a short, de-identified digest, which you review before anything is sent.
  3. Your Psyche Card shows your four letters, how clearly each showed, your Big Five scores and the character you are most like.

Why “MBTI-style”

MBTI is a trademark of the Myers-Briggs Company, and the official instrument is a questionnaire. PsycheAI is not connected to it. It reads the same four preferences from your behaviour instead, and says how confident it is, so a letter that only slightly showed is marked as slight.

Questions

Does it read my friends’ messages?

They are read on your device for timing and context. What is sent is a sample of your own messages, some with a short, de-identified line of what they answered; other people become numbers like [P1].

Is it the same as 16Personalities?

No. 16Personalities is a questionnaire. This is a reading from behaviour, so it can differ from a quiz result; the difference is often the interesting part.

Is it free?

Yes. Your first Psyche Card and Psyche Report are free.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, and your results stay in your browser until you delete them.
