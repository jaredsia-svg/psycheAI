What a Po reading means

A Po reading is someone full of enthusiasm, good humour and big-hearted kindness. They love food, friends and their favourite things with total commitment, and they keep going when others give up. Their warmth wins people over.

You might be most like Po if…

  • Your posts are full of food, friends and fandoms.
  • You are enthusiastic in every chat.
  • You cheer for others generously.
  • You keep trying until it works.
