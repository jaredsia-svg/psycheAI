What a WALL-E reading means

A WALL-E reading is someone who finds wonder in small things and keeps them close. They are patient, a little shy and very loyal, and they show care through what they do rather than what they say. They keep collections, memories and habits that mean a lot to them.

You might be most like WALL-E if…

  • You save and keep things others would scroll past.
  • You post rarely but meaningfully.
  • You stay in touch with a small, steady circle.
  • Your interests run deep rather than wide.

Strengths and what to watch

Strengths

  • Deeply loyal

Worth watching

  • Trying new things a little sooner
