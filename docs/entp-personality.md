# ENTP: The inventive debater

ENTPs love ideas, possibilities and a lively exchange. They are quick, witty and good at seeing angles others miss.

## The four letters

- **E – Extraversion**: energised by people and activity
- **N – Intuition**: drawn to ideas, patterns and possibilities
- **T – Thinking**: decides with logic and consistency first
- **P – Perceiving**: likes open options and adapting as they go

## How ENTP shows up in your data

- Funny, quick comments and replies
- Many interests saved and searched
- Lots of different circles

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Inventive
- Quick-witted
- Adaptable

### Worth watching

- Following through on plans
- Letting a debate rest
