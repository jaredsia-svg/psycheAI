# A Big Five personality test without a questionnaire

The Big Five is the personality model most research uses. PsycheAI scores all five from how you actually behave in your own data, and shows the evidence behind each score.

## The five traits

- **Openness**: curiosity, imagination and appetite for new things.
- **Conscientiousness**: planning, follow-through and reliability.
- **Extraversion**: energy from people and activity, measured by the breadth of people you seek out rather than how much you talk.
- **Agreeableness**: warmth, cooperation and how you handle disagreement.
- **Emotional sensitivity** (often called neuroticism): how strongly you feel stress and worry.

## How behaviour is scored

Each trait is read from patterns, not single posts: how regularly you post and follow through on plans, how many different people you talk to, how you reply when a conversation turns tense, how wide your interests range. Scores run from 0 to 100, and each comes with the evidence that supports it.

## Why not just answer questions?

Questionnaires measure how you see yourself. Behaviour shows what you actually do, which can be different, and more interesting. Both are useful; this one needs no forms and nothing to remember.

### How accurate is a Big Five read from social media?

It is an inference, not a clinical measurement, and it says how confident it is. Accounts with more of your own writing give a clearer read.

### What is the difference between the Big Five and MBTI?

MBTI sorts people into 16 types; the Big Five scores five traits on a scale. PsycheAI shows both.
