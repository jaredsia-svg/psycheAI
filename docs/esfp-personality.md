# ESFP: The life of the party

ESFPs are fun, warm and spontaneous. They love people, experiences and making the moment better.

## The four letters

- **E – Extraversion**: energised by people and activity
- **S – Sensing**: focused on the concrete and practical
- **F – Feeling**: decides with people and values first
- **P – Perceiving**: likes open options and adapting as they go

## How ESFP shows up in your data

- Lots of photos with friends
- Stories and posts from events
- Chatty, emoji-rich messages

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Fun
- Warm
- Spontaneous

### Worth watching

- Planning ahead
- Quiet time to recharge
