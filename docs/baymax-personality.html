What a Baymax reading means

A Baymax reading is someone whose instinct is to care. They notice when people are struggling, ask how they are, and stay calm while others panic. They are not loud about it, but the people around them feel looked after.

You might be most like Baymax if…

  • Your messages often start with how someone is doing.
  • You remember birthdays, exams and hard weeks.
  • Your activity is steady and calm rather than in bursts.
  • You are the friend people go to when things are hard.

Strengths and what to watch

Strengths

  • Caring and attentive
  • Patient and calm
  • Steady and dependable

Worth watching

  • Looking after themselves as well as they look after others
  • Asking for care in return
