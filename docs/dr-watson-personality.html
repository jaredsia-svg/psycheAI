What a Dr Watson reading means

A Dr Watson reading is someone people rely on without having to ask. They look after others, keep good records and stay calm when things get strange. They are often the warm, practical centre of a group, and the one who tells its story afterwards.

You might be most like Dr Watson if…

  • You check in on friends when they go quiet.
  • You keep the photos, the plans and the history of your group.
  • Your messages are practical help as often as chat.
  • You stick with friendships for years.

Strengths and what to watch

Strengths

  • Loyal and dependable
  • Calm and practical
  • Brings out the best in others

Worth watching

  • Taking the lead more often
  • Giving their own ideas as much weight as others’
