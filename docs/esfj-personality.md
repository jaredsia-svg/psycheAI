# ESFJ: The generous host

ESFJs are warm, sociable and focused on making people feel welcome and cared for.

## The four letters

- **E – Extraversion**: energised by people and activity
- **S – Sensing**: focused on the concrete and practical
- **F – Feeling**: decides with people and values first
- **J – Judging**: likes plans, decisions and closure

## How ESFJ shows up in your data

- Lots of photos with friends and family
- Warm comments and quick replies
- Planning gatherings and celebrations

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Warm
- Supportive
- Brings people together

### Worth watching

- Their own needs alongside others’
- Taking criticism less personally
