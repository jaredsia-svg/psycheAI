What a Shrek reading means

A Shrek reading is someone who values their own space, says what they think and has a dry sense of humour. They are slow to let people in, but once they do they are loyal and protective. They prefer real over polished.

You might be most like Shrek if…

  • Your humour is dry and your comments are honest.
  • You keep a small, close circle.
  • You like your own time and space.
  • You are fiercely loyal once someone is in.
