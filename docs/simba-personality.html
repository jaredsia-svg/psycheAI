What a Simba reading means

A Simba reading is someone warm, social and naturally looked to by others. They enjoy the moment and the people in it, and when something matters they step up. They are at their best when they remember the people relying on them and lead with the heart that got them there.

You might be most like Simba if…

  • Your posts are full of friends, groups and good times.
  • People reply to you quickly, and you to them.
  • You lift the mood in group chats.
  • You care a lot about the people who count on you.
