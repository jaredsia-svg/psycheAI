What a Kuromi reading means

A Kuromi reading is someone with a bold style, a mischievous streak and a soft centre. They like doing things their own way, enjoy a bit of attitude and are loyal to the few who get them.

You might be most like Kuromi if…

  • Your style and posts have a distinct edge.
  • You joke with a mischievous streak.
  • You keep a close circle.
  • You show care in your own way.
