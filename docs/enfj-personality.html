ENFJ: The warm motivator

ENFJs bring people together and help them grow. They are warm, articulate and tuned in to how others feel.

The four letters

  • E – Extraversion: energised by people and activity
  • N – Intuition: drawn to ideas, patterns and possibilities
  • F – Feeling: decides with people and values first
  • J – Judging: likes plans, decisions and closure

How ENFJ shows up in your data

  • Posts that celebrate other people
  • Encouraging comments on many accounts
  • Organising group plans
