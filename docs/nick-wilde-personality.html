What a Nick Wilde reading means

A Nick Wilde reading is someone with easy charm, sharp humour and a knack for reading any room. They move comfortably between very different groups and keep things light. Underneath the wit is real loyalty to the few who get to know them well.

You might be most like Nick Wilde if…

  • Your comments and messages are funny and quick.
  • You keep in touch with very different circles.
  • You are relaxed about plans and good at improvising.
  • Your closest chats are where you are most sincere.

Strengths and what to watch

Strengths

  • Socially quick and easy company
  • Adaptable
  • Loyal to their closest people

Worth watching

  • Following through on the plans they make
