What it reads in a chat

  • Who starts conversations, you or them.
  • How quickly each of you replies, as a typical time, not a race.
  • When you are active, by hour and by day of the week.
  • How you write: the length of your messages, how often you ask questions, and a sample of your own words across the life of the chat.

Why one chat adds so much

Instagram shows how you are in public and in your DMs. A WhatsApp chat with someone close is your private voice: how you comfort, plan, tease and disagree. Attachment, conflict and the way you show care depend on that most, so a chat or two sharpens those parts of your reading.

Because you choose the chats, they are usually with your closest people. The reading is told so, and does not take how much you message in them as a sign of how social you are.

What is sent

The chat is read on your device and never uploaded, and you are asked which sender is you. The summary holds the chat’s numbers and a sample of your own messages, some with a short, de-identified line of what they answered. Names become numbers, and links, emails and phone numbers are removed. You review it before anything is sent, and PsycheAI keeps no copy.

How to export a WhatsApp chat

  1. Open the chat and tap ⋮ (or the contact’s name on iPhone), then More → Export chat.
  2. Choose Without media and save the .zip it makes.
  3. Load it in PsycheAI with your Instagram data. You can add up to three chats.

Questions

Do I need Instagram too?

Yes, for now. Your Psyche Card starts from your Instagram data, and WhatsApp chats are added to it. Requesting the Instagram download takes about 30 seconds, and the file usually arrives within 1–2 hours.

Does it read my friend’s messages?

They are read on your device to time the conversation. What is sent is a sample of your own messages, some with a short, de-identified line of what they answered, and your friend’s name becomes a number.

Can I add a group chat?

Yes. A group chat loads the same way, and the reading is told it is a group.
