What a Sailor Moon reading means

A Sailor Moon reading is someone emotional, generous and devoted to their friends. They may seem carefree, but when their people need them they find real courage. They believe in friendship and in second chances.

You might be most like Sailor Moon if…

  • Your posts are full of friends and feelings.
  • You keep in touch with many people warmly.
  • You forgive easily.
  • You show up when friends need you.
