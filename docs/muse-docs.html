PsycheAI in Muse: how it works

What the PsycheAI skill does in your Muse, what it sends us, and how you can check it.

PsycheAI · Singapore · Last updated 4 November 2026 · PsycheAI in Muse

Installing

Paste Install psycheai.io/muse and generate my Psyche into Muse. Muse fetches three files from app.psycheai.io into ~/workspace/skills/psycheai/: SKILL.md (what Muse follows), bin/psyche.py (the script) and guide.json (how a Psyche is written). When Muse asks to share information with app.psycheai.io, tap Always allow this site. There's no sign-up: no email, password or key.

What happens in your Muse

  • Reading. The script reads your Instagram posts, stories and comments through Muse's own Instagram tools, and your busiest chats if you choose. WhatsApp, Facebook or Messenger only if you add them.
  • De-identifying. It removes names, handles, phone numbers, emails and links, and keeps other people's words only as context for your replies, never quoted. What it read is kept as one de-identified file, the digest.
  • Writing. Muse writes your Psyche from the digest, following guide.json, and the script checks it.
  • Your card and report. The script draws your Psyche Card and lays out your report as a file, both in your workspace.

What it sends us

None of your posts, messages or report. The script asks app.psycheai.io only for its own account (no name or email), its menus, signed updates and the art your card is drawn with. If you choose to Sync with a friend, your card's few lines are sent, locked under your Sync link.

Signed updates

Every release is signed, and the script checks the signature against a key built into it before it installs an update. It never installs an older release over a newer one. The code is public at github.com/jaredsia-svg/psycheai-app.

What it never does

It never posts, comments, likes, follows or messages anyone. It never reads Muse's own side chat, and it never guesses your religion, ethnicity, politics, health or height.
