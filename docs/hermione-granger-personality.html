What a Hermione Granger reading means

A Hermione Granger reading is someone curious, organised and determined to do things properly. They read, learn and prepare, and they are the friend who always knows. Their loyalty and sense of fairness run as deep as their knowledge.

You might be most like Hermione Granger if…
