Privacy policy

PsycheAI reads your personality from your own data. Your export, its summary and your report stay on your device. This page says exactly what leaves it, who receives it, and what PsycheAI keeps.

PsycheAI · Singapore · Last updated 9 October 2026

What stays on your device

  • Your Instagram export and any Google, Facebook or WhatsApp files you add are read in your browser. They are never uploaded.
  • Your summary (the “digest”), your Psyche Card and your reports are kept in your browser’s storage. Delete everything on your report page removes them.

What is sent, and to whom

  • The AI provider. To write a card or report, the reduced summary you reviewed is sent to Google (Gemini API) or Anthropic (Claude API). They process it on servers outside Singapore and return the report. The summary does not include your name or username.
  • PsycheAI’s server passes the summary to the provider and the report back. It holds a finished report in memory for up to four hours, so that a dropped connection is not charged twice. It never writes the report to disk.
  • Stripe processes payments. PsycheAI never receives your full card number. It sees the amount, the currency and a payment reference.
  • Render hosts the site and Upstash holds the records listed below. Like any website, they receive your IP address when you connect.
  • People you send your link to see your Psyche Card. They never see your export, messages or full report.

What PsycheAI keeps

  • Daily totals, such as how many cards were made that day. They contain nothing about any one person.
  • A one-way scrambled code of your Instagram username, so each account gets one free card. It cannot be turned back into your username.
  • Your link’s code, and plain counts for it: how many times it was opened, how many friends made a card from it, how many bought the full report, and how many synced with you — never who. Any free full report earned, and any you gift, is kept 60 days.
  • If you share your link: a locked copy of your card’s short summary. Your device locks it, and the key exists only in the link, so PsycheAI cannot read it. It is kept for a year after the link was last opened.
  • Payment references, so a paid report can be retried if it fails.
  • A scrambled code of your IP address, for up to an hour, to limit abuse.

There are no cookies, no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking. PsycheAI does not sell or share data for marketing.

Your rights

Under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act you may ask to access or correct personal data, or withdraw consent. Most of what PsycheAI keeps cannot be linked to you. Email admin@psycheai.io to ask about it, or to have your shared link deleted (send us the link). Deleting the data in your browser is in your own hands.

Other people in your data

Your messages contain other people’s words. Only short samples are included in the summary, private handles are removed from reports, and nothing is shown to anyone but you. Only upload exports of your own accounts.

Age, and changes

PsycheAI is for people aged 18 and over. If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: admin@psycheai.io.
