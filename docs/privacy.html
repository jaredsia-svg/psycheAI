Privacy policy

PsycheAI reads your personality from your own data. Your data and your report stay on your device. This page answers, in plain words, what leaves it, who receives it and what PsycheAI keeps.

PsycheAI · Singapore · Last updated 11 October 2026

Your data

What stays on your device?

Your Instagram zip file, any Google, Facebook or WhatsApp files you add, your photos and videos, and your finished report never leave this device. They are read in your browser. Your digest, Psyche Card and reports stay in your browser until you press Delete everything.

What leaves your device?

Only a short digest of your own activity, usually about 100 KB of text. You see it, and can untick anything, before it goes.

It is de-identified on your device first: your name and username become a blank, email addresses, phone numbers, ID, card and postal numbers are removed, and everyone else’s @handles become numbers like [P1].

Who receives it?

  • Google (Gemini API) or Anthropic (Claude API), to write your card or report, for that one request. They process it on servers outside Singapore.
  • PsycheAI’s server only passes the digest to them and the report back. It does not store, log or show either. If your connection drops, the finished report waits in the server’s memory for up to four hours so you are not charged twice. Only your device can collect it, and it is never saved to disk.
  • Stripe handles payments. PsycheAI never sees your card number, only the amount, the currency and a payment reference.
  • Render hosts the site and Upstash holds the small records below. Like any website, they see your IP address when you connect.
  • People you send your link to see your Psyche Card, never your data, messages or full report.
  • The person whose link you sync with gets the result and your Psyche Card, if you choose to share it, before syncing or later.

Can anyone else access my data?

No. There is no sign-up, no password and no email address. Not even PsycheAI’s administrator can see your data or your results: there is no admin view of them, and nothing from your digest or report is stored or logged.

What PsycheAI keeps

Only a few small things, and none of them names you:

  • Daily totals, such as how many cards were made that day.
  • A scrambled code of your Instagram username, so each account gets one free card. It cannot be turned back into your username.
  • Simple counts for your link: how many times it was opened, how many friends made a card from it, bought the full report or synced with you – never who. A free full report you earn or gift is kept for 60 days.
  • If you share your link, a locked copy of your card’s short summary. Your device locks it and only your link holds the key, so PsycheAI cannot read it. It is kept for a year after the link was last opened.
  • If someone syncs with your link, the result and their Psyche Card, locked the same way with your link’s key, so PsycheAI cannot read them. They are deleted once your device has them, or after 30 days.
  • Payment references, so a paid report can be retried if it fails.
  • A scrambled code of your IP address, for up to an hour, to stop abuse.

There are no cookies, no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking. PsycheAI does not sell or share data.

Your rights and other questions

Can I see, correct or delete my data?

Under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act you can ask to access or correct your personal data, or withdraw consent. Most of what PsycheAI keeps cannot be linked to you. Email admin@psycheai.io to ask, or to have your shared link deleted (send us the link). The data in your browser you can delete yourself, with Delete everything.

What about other people in my messages?

Your messages contain other people’s words. Only short samples go into the digest, other people’s handles are removed, and nothing is shown to anyone but you. Only upload data from your own accounts.

Who can use PsycheAI?

People aged 18 and over. If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: admin@psycheai.io.
