# The best friendship compatibility tests in 2026

The short answer: BFF quiz apps test how well you know each other; MBTI compatibility charts guess from your types alone; love language quizzes compare how you show care. Psyche Sync, from PsycheAI, compares two full personality readings from each person’s own data, for free.

## The list

### 1. BFF quiz apps

One friend writes ten questions about themselves and the other guesses. Fun, fast and free. **Measures:** how well you know each other, not whether you suit each other.

### 2. MBTI compatibility charts

Tables that rate each pair of the 16 types. Free and everywhere. **Measures:** a guess from four letters each, the same for every pair with those types. [The 16 types](https://psycheai.io/mbti-types).

### 3. Love language quizzes

Each of you takes the quiz and compares how you like to give and receive care. **Measures:** one thing, but a useful one. [Love languages without a quiz](https://psycheai.io/love-language-test).

### 4. Psyche Sync (ours)

Each of you makes a free Psyche Card from your own Instagram data, then one link compares them: where you align, where you differ, and what each of you can do for the other, with the evidence from both cards. No score and no verdict, and never romantic. **Measures:** your actual temperaments, side by side. [How Psyche Sync works](https://psycheai.io/best-friend-compatibility-test).

## What makes a friendship test worth taking

- **It looks at you both, not your labels.** Two people of the same type can be very different friends.
- **It explains, rather than scores.** A percentage invites a fight; a difference with a way to handle it is something you can share.
- **It respects your privacy.** Psyche Sync shares only each card’s short summary, never your data or messages.
