# ISTP: The hands-on problem solver

ISTPs are practical, calm and good with how things work. They like to learn by doing and keep their independence.

## The four letters

- **I – Introversion**: energised by time alone and close company
- **S – Sensing**: focused on the concrete and practical
- **T – Thinking**: decides with logic and consistency first
- **P – Perceiving**: likes open options and adapting as they go

## How ISTP shows up in your data

- Saves about tools, sport, cars or making things
- Few posts, short messages
- Activity around hobbies more than socialising

## Strengths and what to watch

### Strengths

- Practical
- Calm under pressure
- Independent

### Worth watching

- Sharing plans with others
- Staying in touch
