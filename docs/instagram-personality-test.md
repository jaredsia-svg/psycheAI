# What does your Instagram say about you?

Most personality tests ask how you think you behave. PsycheAI reads how you actually do: years of what you like, save, search and write on Instagram. You get a free Psyche Card in about a minute, with no questionnaire.

## Why your own data beats a quiz

A questionnaire records the person you think you are on the day you answer it. Your Instagram history records thousands of small choices made when nobody was asking: the hour you are online, what you save and never act on, who you reply to in seconds and who waits a day, the captions you rewrite before posting. Patterns across years are hard to fake, and they are exactly what a personality reading needs.

PsycheAI turns your Instagram data into a reading of you, citing the evidence for each claim, so you can check it against your own life rather than take it on trust.

## How to get your Instagram data

1. In Instagram, open Settings → **Accounts Centre** → **Your information and permissions** → **Download your information**.
2. Choose **Create export** → your account → **Export to device**.
3. Set Date range to **All time**, Format to **JSON** (not HTML) and Media quality to **Lower**.
4. Instagram emails a link, usually within 1–2 hours. Download the .zip and load it into PsycheAI.

Adding your Google Takeout (searches and YouTube) or your Facebook data makes the reading sharper, and is optional.

## What happens to your data

Your Instagram zip, your photos and videos, the finished report and other people’s messages – apart from a short, anonymised line of what some of your messages were replying to – never leave your device. A short digest of your own words and activity is de-identified first (your name becomes a blank; emails, phone and ID numbers are removed; other people’s handles and the names of people you message become numbers like [P1] and [N1]) and is sent once to Google Vertex AI (Gemini, on Google Cloud’s enterprise tier, which does not train on it) to write the reading. PsycheAI keeps no copy, and there is no account – only a one-way scrambled code of your Instagram username, so each account gets one free card.

## What each part of your data says

- **Your captions and comments** are your own words: what you celebrate, how you write to people, what you announce and then follow through on.
- **Your messages** show how you keep in touch: who starts conversations, how warmly you reply, how you handle a tense moment.
- **When you are active**, hour by hour and week by week, shows your rhythm: steady or in bursts, mornings or late nights.
- **What you like, save and search** shows where your attention goes when nobody is watching, which can differ from what you post.

None of these alone says much. Together, over years, they are a detailed picture, which is why the reading cites its evidence for every finding. ## Questions

### Is an Instagram personality test accurate?

It is an inference from behaviour, not a clinical measurement or a diagnosis. Every finding names the evidence behind it and the card carries a confidence score, so you can see how much there was to go on. The more years of activity in your export, the more there is to read.

### Do I have to log in with Instagram?

No. PsycheAI never asks for your Instagram password or connects to your account. You download your own data from Instagram and load the file yourself.

### How long does it take?

Instagram usually prepares your export within 1–2 hours. Once you have the file, your Psyche Card and Psyche Report take a few minutes.
