What a Hello Kitty reading means

A Hello Kitty reading is someone gentle, warm and thoughtful toward friends. They love small gestures, cute things and making others smile. They are easy to like and keep friendships going with care.

You might be most like Hello Kitty if…

  • Your posts are sweet, colourful and friendly.
  • You send kind messages and small gifts.
  • You remember what friends like.
  • You keep in touch with lots of people.
